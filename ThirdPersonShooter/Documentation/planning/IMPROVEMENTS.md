# Ideas to make the port better than the Godot original (NOT done on purpose)

Port first, like-for-like; these are deliberate differences to consider once the slice matches Godot.

1. **Bullets ignore "Player Only" props.** Godot bullet `collision_mask = 3` (Level + Player) vs prop/railing StaticBodies on `collision_layer = 4` (Player Only, e.g. `props.tscn:107-109`, Props50 near spawn at 69.95,-0.2,76.65). So lasers pass through ~54 prop boxes and ~79 railing/pillar colliders and the radial PlayerOnly ones, then impact behind them. Fix later: let bullets (and the aim ray) also hit layer 4, or a dedicated bullet-blocking subset of props; keep the Godot behaviour behind a flag. Found 2026-11-17 by the user.
2. **Real shadows.** The plan only has a blob shadow under the player (and a baked shadow on the menu floor). Godot has real shadow mapping (Settings: shadow mapping on/off) on the level and the robots. Proposed as its own pass after the lighting contrast pass.
3. **Positional audio panning.** 3D sounds are attenuated but centred because OpenAL does not pan stereo buffers; add a mono-downmix content processor so Pan/Apply3D is audible, and let robot sounds follow the robot.

4. **Player health and damage (gameplay design change).** Godot has none: `player.gd:205` `hit()` only adds camera shake (0.75), a robot laser hit adds 13.0 trauma (`red_robot.gd:134-135`), and only robots have health (5). Proposal: give the player health, with a hit reaction (shake, red vignette, hit sound, optional flinch), damage from robot lasers and, in multiplayer, from other players' bullets (the bullet mask already hits the Player layer, `bullet.tscn:484`), and a death/respawn flow (the fall respawn at y < -40 already exists). Open numbers to decide: max health, damage per bullet/laser, regeneration, respawn delay and invulnerability. Needs a health HUD and a settings-neutral way to keep the like-for-like mode (e.g. a "Classic" toggle).
5. **Multiplayer as a deathmatch.** Follows from item 4: Godot multiplayer is a shared sandbox (players only shake each other). With health, kills, deaths and a scoreboard, LAN multiplayer (planned, modelled on RacingGame) becomes a deathmatch: per-player score, respawn at a random spawn marker (Godot already shuffles the 4 player markers), kill feed, match limit/timer. Bullets are server-simulated in Godot, so damage should be server-authoritative.
6. **Powerups.** New content, not in Godot. Candidates: health pack, damage boost, rapid fire, shield, speed boost, and a laser-resistance pickup against the robots. Open design questions: pickup placement (fixed spawn points vs random), respawn timers, effect durations, and visuals/audio (reuse the glow and light effects). Depends on items 4 and 5; keep out of the like-for-like single-player mode unless the user asks.
7. **DONE 2026-11-18 (software; hardware feel unverified) - Gamepad rumble.** Neither Godot nor the port uses force feedback (gamepad itself confirmed working 2026-11-17). Idea: `GamePad.SetVibration` tied to the camera-shake events (short pulse on firing, strong pulse on a laser hit, the 13.0 trauma). Untested with SDL on macOS; make it a Settings toggle.
   - Done: `Core/Input/RumbleService.cs` (sink delegate, headless-tested), `CameraShake.TraumaAdded` event, Settings > RUMBLE (default ON, `GameSettings.Rumble`). Fire 0.35 = low motor 0.25 for 0.12 s; hit 0.75 = 0.6/0.5 for 0.2 s; laser 13 = 0.9/0.9 for 0.35 s; linear decay, strongest wins; zeroed on pause, menu, loading, focus loss, exit, disconnect, toggle off; no sink calls without a pad or with RUMBLE off. The port has no player hit() path today; the 0.75 tier is only mapped, not triggered. Test: `--test-ui` 9/9. Shot mono-58.
   - SDL on macOS: startup log `[rumble] pad connected=.. vibration supported=..` (the pad state varied between two launches in the same session: first False, later True/True with SetVibration returning True). `TPS_RUMBLETEST=1 ... --sim "aim;wait:0.2;fire:1;wait:1"` runs the sim in real time and logs every SetVibration call with a timestamp. Whether the motors physically move is NOT verified; the user must feel it with their pad.
8. **Optimisation pass (required before release; schedule a dedicated agent after lighting pass 6).** Baseline 2026-11-17 (Release, macOS arm64, 1280x720, no vsync, `--bench 300`): ~20.7 ms avg / ~25 ms p95 per frame with key-light shadows ON (~20.0 ms OFF), 2914 draw calls, 2.25M triangles, 970 shadow-caster draws. Scope to consider: frustum/occlusion culling for the main pass (no frustum culling on the level today), batching or instancing of repeated props to cut draw calls, merging static meshes by material, texture memory (2048 cap, DXT already), shadow caster culling and map size, bloom/post cost and render-target formats, per-frame allocations (GC) in the sim/effects/particles, load-time cost (cold cache is slower than the measured ~1.6 s to first menu frame), and a quality preset in Settings (shadows, bloom, resolution scale). Measure first with `--bench` (3 runs, avg and p95), set a target (for example <= 16.6 ms avg on the dev machine), and report before/after per change. Re-run the self-tests and the lighting metrics after each step so the look does not drift.
9. **Robot power-up and laser fire effects (polish phase before release).** Effects part 2 (commit 6bc7dcbd, crash fix d7ad073a) is "better than nothing", not final. To improve: the robot charge-up (buildup particles) and the laser beam look: beam thickness and brightness (the tube radius/length are assumed because `raymesh.mesh` is binary; gain 1.2 is a guess), noise stripes, the laser impact effect (smoke uses `blue_myst` as a stand-in for `SmokePuff.png`; `BlastMesh_Sphere` and `LightRaysMesh` sizes assumed; `LaserCenter`, red_robot.tscn:10725: DONE 2026-11-18 [x], white-red guess, material binary), the barrel smoke, muzzle sparks (about 2 cm, barely visible) and the bullet-hit cloud (very large and bright at point-blank range, overdraw cost at retina resolution never measured; consider capping its size near the camera). [x] Forklift exhaust + SpotLight3D DONE 2026-11-18 (effects part 3; colours/gain guessed, projector texture not used). There is no Godot reference frame of the robot laser (godot-03/04 show the player's bullets): capture one from the Godot project to compare. Verify with a live `--sim` (no `--shot`) as well as stills, because the last crash only showed up in real-time play.
10. **Standing-light "flicker on" as a feature.** Spot shadows are tied to the 3 nearest level spots with a 0.35 s fade, so distant tripod lights visibly switch on as the player approaches (user, 2026-11-18: "not necessarily a bad thing"). Idea: make it deliberate: lights flicker on (short irregular strobe, a click/hum sound, a brief emissive pop on the lamp head) when the player comes within a trigger distance (for example 14-26 m), and optionally off again when leaving. Keep Godot's always-on look behind a Settings toggle; budget stays at 3 shadowed spots.
11. **Random player spawn: multiplayer only.** Godot shuffles the 4 player markers even in single player. The port keeps a fixed spawn in single player (repeatable sims, screenshots and tests, user decision 2026-11-18). Do the random/shuffled marker pick as part of the multiplayer work (item 5), so players do not start on top of each other.
12. **Gas bottle overlap (polish pass).** The orange-and-white gas bottle next to the blue crate near spawn still shows a grey/orange overlap when moving. It is probably level data, but not yet compared against the Godot node transforms. Revisit in the polish pass before release.
13. **LAN multiplayer (polish pass / after gameplay).** Not started. Model it on RacingGame LAN; Godot's version is server-authoritative ENet. Revisit after the gameplay items (health, deathmatch) are decided, so the netcode is not touched twice. Revisit in the polish pass.
