# Catapult Wars: it's not just left and right any more

Same two catapults, same home network, but this update changes how every shot feels. Here's what's new.

## Aim it, don't just fling it

- **Pull back to aim.** Drag back from your catapult like a slingshot. How far you pull sets the power, and which way you pull sets the angle.
- **Angle and Power readouts** sit under each player. They show the numbers of the last shot, and update live while you pull, so you know exactly what to change on the next one.
- **See the AI aim.** The AI now draws its arrow too, and you can watch it settle on a shot before it fires. It's also been dialled back a little, so it misses about as often as you do.

## A battlefield you can change

- **A hill in the middle**, and every shot that lands digs a **crater**, in the hill as well as the ground. Lobbing over it or tunnelling through it is up to you.
- **A new layout every game.** The hill's height and shape change, and so does the distance between the catapults. On a LAN game the host decides, so both screens show exactly the same battlefield.
- **A crew member** stands beside each catapult. They cheer when their side scores and shake both fists when the other side does.

## Feels better

- **Slow-mo zoom** when a shot is about to hit a catapult, and a **little camera shake** when it lands.
- **Smoother animations.** They now run on time rather than frames, so there's no more jerkiness when the frame rate wobbles.
- **Esc goes back** a screen, and menus wrap around when you press up or down past the end.
- **A new icon:** the blue catapult, with its crew.
- **Player names** no longer show the odd `_xxxxxxxx` ending in the lobby.

## Fixed

- **Catapult vanishing at full power.** At 95% power or above the catapult disappeared and two odd lines were drawn.
- **LAN: the second player's shot fired by itself.** The player joining a game fired the moment they started to drag. They don't now.

## Download

Windows, macOS (installer), Linux and Android builds are on the page. For LAN games, both players should be on this version, because the battlefield layout is shared between them.

Thanks to everyone who has played so far, and especially those who took the time to tell me what felt wrong. Keep it coming.
