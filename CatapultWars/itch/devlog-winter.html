Halloween was fun, so there's a new look for December, and a few things that make every look feel more alive.

Winter is coming (1 to 31 December)

From 1 December the battlefield turns wintry on its own. Nothing to switch on.

  • Snow everywhere. A dusk sky, a big pale moon, frosted hills and snow lying on the ground.
  • Snowballs instead of rocks. They puff into white powder when they land.
  • Craters dig through the snow. Every hit scoops the snow away and shows the brown earth underneath, with a little rim of snow left round the edge.
  • Santa hats for both crews, and coloured lights twinkling on the trees.
  • A red-nosed reindeer on the far hillside. It keeps an eye on the battle, and stoops to graze now and then.

If winter isn't your thing, the Seasonal Theme switch on the main menu turns it off. Each player sees their own look, so one of you can play in the snow while the other keeps the classic view.

Wind you can see

  • Things blow on the wind in every look, in the same direction and with the same strength that pushes your shot: leaves in the classic look, embers in Halloween and snow in winter. Check which way they're heading before you pull back.
  • It's all drawn without allocating anything while you play, so it shouldn't make the game stutter.

The reindeer joined every look

  • The reindeer isn't only for Christmas. It's on the far hill in the classic look (a plain one), and in Halloween it's haunted, with flaming antlers.

Fixed

  • "1/15" in the join list. A game only ever has two players, but the list made it look like fifteen could join. It now says 1/2, and a third machine can no longer slip into the lobby.
  • The window and the app are called "Catapult Wars" now, not the name of the project.

Also

  • Catapults now stay on the victory screen, and long player names shrink to fit their box instead of running off the edge.
