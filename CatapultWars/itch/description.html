Make LAN Games Great Again.

One router. Two catapults. Zero accounts, zero servers, zero subscriptions.
Catapult Wars is a head-to-head siege duel you play with the person sitting on the same Wi-Fi as you.

Remember when "online multiplayer" meant dragging a PC to a mate's house, plugging in a cable and shouting across the room when you landed a hit? That is this game. Open it on two machines on your home network, one of you hosts, the other joins, and the trash talk begins.

Why LAN? Why now?

  • No sign-up. No launcher, no login, no friend codes. If you're on the same network, you see each other.
  • No lag-switching strangers. Your opponent is your sibling, your kid, your flatmate, your dad. You know where they live.
  • No servers to die. There is nothing to shut down in five years. The game runs on your hardware and your network, and it always will.
  • No data harvested. The only thing leaving your router is the sound of someone yelling "THAT WIND WAS NOT FAIR".
  • Same room, same couch, same snacks. The best latency is the distance across the sofa.

How a match goes

  1. Pick Play, then Multi Player on LAN from the main menu. Catapult Wars uses System Link: it finds games on your local network automatically.
  2. Host or Join. One person creates a game; the other picks it from the list of games found on the LAN.
  3. Ready up in the lobby. The host can also pick how long the match is: first to 5, 10 or 15 hits.
  4. Take turns firing. Set your angle and power, read the wind, let it fly.
  5. First to reach the points target wins (5 by default). Then immediately demand a rematch.

The game

  • Turn-based artillery duel: easy to learn in 30 seconds, hard to stop playing.
  • Wind changes every turn, so last round's perfect shot is this round's embarrassing miss.
  • Hand-animated catapults with pull-back, fire, smoke and destruction animations.
  • Short matches, ideal for "one more game" before dinner.
  • Play solo against the AI to practise before you take on the family champion.

Great for

  • Parents and kids who want a game that isn't a feed
  • Siblings settling a very serious dispute
  • Flatmates and housemates
  • LAN party warm-up rounds
  • Anyone who misses "come over, bring your laptop"
  • Anyone who hates adverts: there are none, not even a "quick" one between rounds
  • People sick of games guilt-tripping them for not playing every day: no streaks, no daily login rewards, no "we miss you!" notifications. Come back in a week or a year; the catapults will be waiting.

What you need

  • Two computers (or more devices on the platforms you build for) on the same local network
  • Wi-Fi or Ethernet. No internet connection required to play.
  • If the other player doesn't appear, check that your firewall allows the game on private networks, and that your router doesn't have "client isolation" / "AP isolation" switched on (common on guest networks).

Open source, built with MonoGame

Catapult Wars is a MonoGame sample targeting .NET 10, with desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux), Android and iOS projects, and uses MonoGame.Xna.Framework.Net to bring back the classic XNA-style network sessions. Read the code, mod it, port it, make your own LAN game. That's rather the point.
